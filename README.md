# Tech Firm and Institutional Ownership
This folder includes empirical analysis results for Sirin & Yilmaz (2024) paper on tech firms and institutional ownership.

Sirin, Selahattin Murat and YILMAZ, Berna N., Tech Firms and the Renewable Energy Sector: Exploring the Moderating Effects of Institutional Ownership on Financial Connectedness. Available at SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=4981696 or http://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4981696
